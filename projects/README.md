## Why some projects live elsewhere

A project moves to its own repo once it's substantial enough to have a real, ongoing commit history worth showing on its own — for example the home lab, which will accumulate its own configs, diagrams, and screenshots over time. Smaller reference material and single-sitting exercises stay here as folders.
